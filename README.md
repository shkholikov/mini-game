# Осман, Сафия и Умар

A 3D browser game for three kids: **Осман** races cars, **Сафия** feeds animals their favourite fruit, and **Умар** (1.5) drives a blue tractor past farm animals: every tap honks, no rules, no end. Russian voice, made for a tablet in landscape.

Умар's background song isn't part of the game. Hold the home button in his mode for 2 seconds, tap «Песня» and pick an mp3 on the tablet; it is kept in that browser only.

Play: https://shkholikov.github.io/mini-game/
